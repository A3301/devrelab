TREN

Every week: pre-lab questions, 3–5 experiments with realistic bench instruments (power supply, multimeter, function generator, oscilloscope), automatically ticked lab-sheet steps, measurement tables, graphs and a printable report. No internet needed; just copy the folder to a USB stick and open index.html.

First time here? The lab in 4 steps

  1. Choose a week and work through the menu in order: Background → Pre-lab → Experiments → Report.
  2. Instrument knobs: drag a knob up/down or use the − / + buttons below it. For an exact value, tap the knob and type the value (e.g. 4.7k, 600µ) or pick it from the list.
  3. The Next step bar at the bottom of the screen tells you what to do. A step is ticked ✓ automatically when you do it correctly.
  4. Add measurements to the table with Record (R), answer the questions, and in the last section print / save the report as PDF.

If an instrument shows nothing: is its output on (OUTPUT ON), is the right connection/mode selected, does the oscilloscope need AUTO or SINGLE? The ? button on every page opens the help window.
